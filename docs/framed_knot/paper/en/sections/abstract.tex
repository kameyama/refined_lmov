\begin{abstract}
Kameyama and Nawata rewrote refined Chern--Simons invariants of knots in terms of refined BPS invariants of the resolved conifold
and conjectured that the latter are non-negative integers; the conjecture was tested only at framing zero.
We extend this framework to framed knots: the invariant at framing $x$ is defined by multiplying the refined Chern--Simons invariant coloured by $\lambda$ by $\theta_\lambda^x$,
where $\theta_\lambda$ is the inverse of the framing factor of the refined topological vertex.
We show that the refined LMOV generating functions are exponential polynomials in $x$,
that the transposition symmetry and the symmetry under which the genus expansion closes do not depend on $x$,
and that for two boxes the Laurent property does not depend on $x$ either and requires the Mari\~no--Vafa sign.
We then test the positivity of the refined LMOV invariants for further knots and framings.
For the fundamental representation, positivity holds for every knot and every framing whenever the input is the Poincar\'e polynomial of reduced HOMFLY-PT homology.
For two boxes, taking the conjectural formulae for coloured superpolynomials as input, we give computer-assisted proofs of positivity for all twist knots and, for eight knots, for all integer framings.
For all representations with at most three boxes we check positivity for seven torus knots and seven algebraic iterated torus knots, also under changes of framing;
for the iterated torus knots these tests rest on a hypothesis that fixes the monomial normalisation of the coloured invariants.
For eight torus knots the checks extend to four or more boxes.
On this basis we formulate positivity conjectures for framed torus knots and for algebraic iterated torus knots.
\end{abstract}
