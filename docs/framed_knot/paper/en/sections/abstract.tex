\begin{abstract}
Kameyama and Nawata rewrote refined Chern--Simons invariants of knots in terms of refined BPS invariants of the resolved conifold
and conjectured that the latter are non-negative integers; the conjecture was tested for torus knots and links and for a few thin knots, all at framing zero.
We extend this framework to framed knots.
The invariant at framing $x$ is defined by multiplying the refined Chern--Simons invariant coloured by $\lambda$ by $\theta_\lambda^x$,
where $\theta_\lambda$ is the inverse of the framing factor of the refined topological vertex and reduces to the Mari\~no--Vafa rule at $t=q$.
We show that the refined LMOV generating functions are exponential polynomials in $x$,
that the transposition symmetry, the Laurent property and the closure of the genus expansion do not depend on $x$,
and that the Mari\~no--Vafa sign is necessary for the Laurent property.
We then test the positivity of the refined LMOV invariants, before and after the genus expansion, beyond the original data, both in the direction of knots and in the direction of framings.
For the fundamental representation, positivity holds for every knot and every framing whenever the input is the Poincar\'e polynomial of reduced HOMFLY-PT homology;
we confirm this for all $801$ prime knots with at most $11$ crossings and relate the parity of the spin to the parity knots of Chandler and Gorsky.
For two boxes, we prove that the exponential growth property is necessary for the Laurent property,
and, taking the conjectural formulae for coloured superpolynomials as input, we give computer-assisted proofs of positivity for all twist knots,
for a large region of double twist knots, and, for eight knots, for all integer framings.
For three boxes, using the refined invariants defined by the elliptic Hall algebra and the double affine Hecke algebra,
we check positivity for all six representations with at most three boxes for seven torus knots and seven algebraic iterated torus knots,
including a three-strand cable and a two-step iteration with about $1.8$ million terms;
positivity persists under changes of framing in all $1821$ cases examined.
For eight torus knots we check positivity from four up to nine boxes ($340$ pairs of a knot and a representation).
On this basis we formulate positivity conjectures for framed torus knots and for algebraic iterated torus knots.
We also discuss the obstruction to treating hyperbolic knots with non-rectangular colours,
and show that the Kameyama--Nawata candidate for the figure-eight knot coloured by $\yng(2,1)$ is obtained from the $Z$-factor expansion of Anokhina et al.\ by changing a single coefficient.
\end{abstract}
