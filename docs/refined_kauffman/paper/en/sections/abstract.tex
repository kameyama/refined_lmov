\begin{abstract}
The large $N$ duality for the colored Kauffman invariant of $\SO(N)$ Chern--Simons theory \cite{Marino09} expresses the difference between the $\SO(N)$ invariant and one half of the $\U(N)$ composite invariant in terms of integer BPS invariants of crosscap number $c = 1, 2$. We refine this duality in the framework of the orientifold refined topological string of \cite{AgSc12}. The refined crosscap invariant is defined as $g = f - \tfrac12 f^{\cov}$, where $f$ is the free energy built from the $\SO(2N)$ refined Chern--Simons invariants with the dual Cauchy kernel of Koornwinder polynomials (a t-brane), and $f^{\cov}$ is the corresponding free energy of the $\U(2N-1)$ cover in composite representations; both are taken in a uniform normalization fixed by determinant shifts and charge conjugation. We then establish the following. (i) From the M-theory index of the orientifold, the all-$d$ structure $g = \tfrac12 E/(1+t) + C$ follows, where $E$ is the signed sum over states invariant under the orientifold involution and $C$ the sum over frozen states; $E$ appears only in sectors with equal numbers of brane and image boxes, and its boundary factor $z_q^{n-1}(z_q^+)^n$ is derived from the action of the involution on boundary zero modes. (ii) Using DAHA-Jones polynomials of type $D_N$ and $GL_N$ at generic $t$, we compute the refined crosscap invariants at general $N$ for the trefoil up to four boxes, $T(2,5)$ up to three boxes, $T(2,7)$ and $T(2,9)$ with three boxes, and---extending the definition to iterated torus knots through the DAHA of Cherednik--Danilenko---for the algebraic $(2,13)$- and $(2,15)$-cables of the trefoil. They are integer Laurent polynomials, all $N$-dependent poles cancel, and the refined part is divisible by the universal factor $(\bt^2 - 1)(\ba^2 - \bq^2)$ in every case. (iii) For the fundamental representation of two-strand torus knots $T(2,2m+1)$, $m \le 5$, the $c = 1$ part admits a $G$-expansion with uniform signs in each $\lambda^\beta$ block and the $c = 2$ part is $-(\ba^2 - \bq^2)(\bt^2 - 1)$ times a positive polynomial; this criterion fails for $T(3,4)$ and for the cables, where the $c = 1$ part is not $\sigma$-symmetric. (iv) Splitting the cover into brane and image, the brane--brane sector coincides with the refined LMOV invariants of \cite{KN17}, and the brane--image sector is identified as a space of states built from $\Sym^k V$, $S \otimes S$ and a universal correction, for fourteen torus knots. (v) The refined $\Sp(2N)$ invariants of \cite{AM22} satisfy $g^{\Sp}(\ba) = -g^{\SO}(-\ba)$ in the fundamental representation and $W_R^{\Sp}(\ba) = (-1)^{|R|}W_R^{\SO}(-\ba)$ with two boxes. For higher representations the $c = 1$ part of the crosscap invariant is not $\sigma$-symmetric and the signs do not align; since the cover is well behaved, the obstruction is localized in the multiplet structure of the frozen states, which we formulate as an open problem.
\end{abstract}
