\section{Refined \texorpdfstring{$\Sp(2N)$}{Sp(2N)}: fundamental representation and two boxes}\label{sec:sp}
The refined Chern--Simons theory of \cite{AM22} is defined for any root system by taking $k_\alpha = y$ uniformly and $q_\alpha = q^{(\alpha,\alpha)/2}$; for $C_N$ the parameter of the long roots becomes $u = t_{\mathrm{lng}} = t^2$.
We implemented the $C_n$ DAHA of \cite{Cherednik11} ($t = t_{\mathrm{sht}}$, $u = t_{\mathrm{lng}}$; see the section on Kauffman polynomials in that paper) on the polynomial representation and verified the following.
% [D11]
\begin{result}\label{cr:cn}
For the $C_N$ DAHA and its hyper-polynomials the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The DAHA relations hold for $C_2$, $C_3$. At $u = 1$ the values agree exactly with the $D_N$ DAHA-Jones polynomials in the fundamental representation ($N = 2, 3$; $T(2,3)$, $T(2,5)$). They agree with $\mathcal{H}^C_{3,2}(\omega_1; q, t, u, a_{\mathrm{C}})$ of \cite{Cherednik11} at $a_{\mathrm{C}} = -t^{N-1}$ up to a monomial (rank $N = 2, 3, 4$).
\item From the values at $N = 2, \dots, 8$ we reconstructed the hyper-polynomials $\mathcal{H}^C_{2m+1,2}(\omega_1)$ as polynomials in $a_{\mathrm{C}}$. The formula for $T(2,5)$ does not appear in \cite{Cherednik11} or \cite{CE15}.
\item $\SO$ convention: $W_{\SO} = \text{mirror}[\mathcal{H}^C(u{=}1, a_{\mathrm{C}}{=}-\lambda/t^{1/2})]\cdot\dim_V^{\SO}(\lambda)\cdot(t^{1/2}/\lambda)^{2m}$ reproduces $g^{\mathrm{true}}_{\yone} = W_{\SO} - W^{\cov}_{\yone}$ (uniquely).
\item $\Sp$: $\lambda = t^{N+1/2}$ ($a = \lambda^2 = t^{2N+1}$; the cover is taken as the same function of $\lambda$ as for $\SO$), $a_{\mathrm{C}} = -t^{N-1}$, $u = t^2$. The monomial is fixed to the same $(t^{1/2}/\lambda)^{2m}$ as for $\SO$ by the cancellation of the pole at $t = 1$ and the Kauffman form at $t = q$, and for $T(2,3)$, $T(2,5)$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sp1}
g^{\Sp}_{\yone}(\ba, \bq, \bt) = -\,g^{\SO}_{\yone}(-\ba, \bq, \bt)
\end{equation}
holds coefficient by coefficient.
\end{enumerate}
\end{result}
Since $c = 1$ is assigned to even and $c = 2$ to odd powers of $\ba$, \eqref{eq:sp1} says $C^{\Sp} = -C_{c=1} + C_{c=2}$: the rule of \cite{Marino09} that $\SO$ and $\Sp$ differ by the sign of the $c = 1$ terms survives refinement. This independently supports the assignment of $c$, and is the first check that the refined $\Sp(2N)$ theory of \cite{AM22} is consistent for open strings. Relation \eqref{eq:sp1} and \eqref{eq:sp2} below are refined versions of the classical correspondence $\Sp(N) = \SO(-N)$ with $R \to R^T$ \cite{Cvitanovic08,MV10}; whether they follow from a duality of the $C_N$ and $D_N$ DAHA at the level of Koornwinder polynomials has not been examined \open.

The same structure holds with two boxes. The $C_N$ Macdonald polynomials $P_{2\varepsilon_1}$, $P_{\varepsilon_1+\varepsilon_2}$ were obtained from the triangular matrix of the Macdonald operator, and the values at $N = 2, \dots, 9$ were interpolated as rational functions of $x = t^N$ (at $u = 1$ they agree with the $D_N$ two-box values and with $\mathcal{H}^C_{3,2}(\omega_2)$ of \cite{Cherednik11} up to a monomial).
% [D12][D13][D14]
\begin{result}\label{cr:sp2}
For the refined $\Sp(2N)$ trefoil with two boxes the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $g^{\Sp}_{\ytwo} + g^{\Sp}_{\yoneone}$ and $(1+t)(g^{\Sp}_{\ytwo} - g^{\Sp}_{\yoneone})$ are integer Laurent polynomials, with the same all-$d$ structure as for $\SO$ (Proposition \ref{prop:alld}).
\item $E^{\Sp}(t{=}-1) = E^{\SO}(t{=}-1)$ coefficient by coefficient.
\item The hatted $p_1^2$ component $(g_{\ytwo} + g_{\yoneone})/z_q$ satisfies $\Sp(\ba) = +\,\SO(-\ba)$ (the fundamental representation has $-$). For $g_R$ itself
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sp2}
\begin{aligned}
f^{\Sp}_{\ytwo}(\ba) - f^{\SO}_{\ytwo}(-\ba) &= \psi^2[\Delta], \qquad f^{\Sp}_{\yoneone}(\ba) - f^{\SO}_{\yoneone}(-\ba) = -\psi^2[\Delta], \\
\Delta &= \mathrm{even}_{\ba}\big(g^{\SO}_{\yone}\big) + 1 = \frac{(\ba^2 - \bq^2)(\ba^2\bt^2 - 1)(\ba^2\bq^2\bt^2 - 1)}{\ba^6\bq^2\bt^4}
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
(the same for $g$; $\psi^2$ is the Adams operation squaring $q, t, \ba^2$, and $\mathrm{even}_{\ba}$ denotes the part with even powers of $\ba$).
\end{enumerate}
\end{result}
In the power-sum components $Z_\rho$ of the generating function ($\rho = (1), (1,1), (2)$) one finds $Z_1^{\Sp}(\ba) = -Z_1^{\SO}(-\ba)$, $Z_{11}^{\Sp}(\ba) = Z_{11}^{\SO}(-\ba)$, $Z_2^{\Sp}(\ba) = Z_2^{\SO}(-\ba) + 1$: the sign is $(-1)^{|\rho|}$ and only the $p_2$ direction is shifted by the constant $1$. In the difference $\psi^2[\Delta]$ of $f$, the piece $\psi^2[\mathrm{even}(g_{\yone})]$ arises because the rule for the fundamental representation flips only the sign of the $\ba$-even ($c = 1$) part, whereas the even part of the $d = 2$ Adams term $\tfrac12\psi^2[f_{\yone}]$ does not follow this rule. The remaining $\psi^2[1]$ is the constant shift of $Z_2$. At the level of colored invariants, $W_R^{\Sp}(\ba) = (-1)^{|R|}\,W_R^{\SO}(-\ba)$ holds exactly, and the conversion to power sums differs only in the constant term of $p_2$ (the trace of the identity) by $-2$; this is the constant $1$ in $Z_2$, equal to the $\ba$-even part of the unknot crosscap in the fundamental representation ($+1$ for $\SO$, $-1$ for $\Sp$).
The two-box computation is restricted to the trefoil ($T(2,5)$ would need $N \le 13$ for the interpolation, beyond the current implementation).
That $E$ does not change sign under the projection to $\Sp$ is consistent with the unrefined correspondence: using $E_{\ytwo} = -E_{\yoneone}$, the sign flip and the transposition cancel. In the $p_2$ direction the Adams term $\psi^2[g_{\yone}]$ of the fundamental representation enters, and since $\psi^2$ ($\lambda \to \lambda^2$) does not commute with $\ba \to -\ba$, there is no simple rule at the level of $f$ and $g$.
