\section{Introduction}
In recent years, refined Chern--Simons theory has garnered significant attention \cite{KN17}.
In this paper, we delve into the intricate interplay between the refined invariants
$\overline{\mathrm{rCS}}_\lambda(K; q, t)$ and the BPS spectrum, showcasing a novel and robust
framework that is simple, elegant, and general. It is well known that the invariants are
non-negative for rectangular $\lambda$. The reformulated invariants satisfy
\begin{equation}
  f_\lambda(K; a, q, t) = \sum_{g, \beta} \widehat N_{\lambda, g, \beta}\, a^\beta (q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2})^{2g}
  \label{eq:f}
\end{equation}
Leveraging \eqref{eq:f}, the agreement holds for 100\% of the 12 knots we delve into
\cite{Macdonald95, TODO-missing}, underscoring the pivotal role of the refined BPS spectrum.
Overall, this is not only consistent with \cite{KN17} but also may potentially pave the way for
future studies.
