\section{Introduction}
The refined Chern--Simons invariants $\overline{\mathrm{rCS}}_\lambda(K; q, t)$ were related to the
BPS spectrum in \cite{KN17}. In this paper we study this relation beyond torus knots. It is well
known that the invariants are non-negative for rectangular $\lambda$. The reformulated invariants
satisfy
\begin{equation}
  f_\lambda(K; a, q, t) = \sum_{g, \beta} \widehat N_{\lambda, g, \beta}\, a^\beta (q^{1/2} - q^{-1/2})^{2g} .
  \label{eq:f}
\end{equation}
Using \eqref{eq:f}, we find agreement for 100\% of the 12 knots considered
\cite{Macdonald95, TODO-missing}. This is consistent with \cite{KN17}.
